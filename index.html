A Sanctuary of Harmony, Peace, & Fellowship

Mongsen Lenden is more than just a geographic colony; it represents a close-knit family bound by mutual respect, shared spiritual values, and a rich historical legacy. Nestled in Nagaland, our community stands as a beacon of togetherness and hospitality.

From generational stories passed down by elders to the youthful energy of our modern community gatherings, Mongsen Lenden harmoniously bridges traditional Ao Naga heritage with progressive community developments.

Spiritual Center

The Colony Church

The **Mongsen Lenden Colony Church** stands as the spiritual sanctuary and moral heart of our community. It is a place where families gather every Sunday, youth lift their voices in praise, and the community comes together in prayer and fellowship.

Beyond weekly worship services, the church nurtures character, hosts youth ministry activities, choir practices, and community counseling. Its open doors symbolize safety, comfort, and the enduring love of God across generations.

Sacred Historic Landmark

First Christian Baptism Pool

The **First Christian Baptism Pool** is a profoundly sacred monument marking the dawn of Christianity in our region. This historic site holds deep spiritual significance as the very waters where pioneer believers publicly confessed their faith in Jesus Christ.

Representing spiritual rebirth, transformation, and the courage of early believers, the baptism pool stands today as a treasured testimonial site. It serves as a living reminder of our salvation heritage and the eternal grace that transformed our people.
